ADVISORY ID: ADV-2099-0031
TITLE: Vulnerability reported in internal scheduler service

SUMMARY:
A vulnerability has been reported in "AcmeScheduler" version 2.4, an internally
developed job scheduling service. Exploitation may allow an authenticated user
to escalate privileges within the scheduler.

AFFECTED:
  - AcmeScheduler 2.4

REMEDIATION:
A patched build (2.4.1) is being prepared by the platform team.

REFERENCES:
  - Internal ticket SEC-4501
